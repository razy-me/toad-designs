## APCA & Contrast Rules
- **Text < 24px**: Must have APCA $|L_c| \ge 75$ AND WCAG $\ge 4.5:1$.
- **Dark Backgrounds** (`#030712` .. `#1e293b`):
  - ❌ NEVER use `#64748b`, `#94a3b8`, or `#38bdf8` for small text ($|L_c| < 65$).
  - ✔ USE `#f8fafc`, `#e2e8f0`, `#bae6fd`, `#6ee7b7`, or `#fde68a` ($|L_c| \ge 84$).
- **Light Backgrounds** (`#ffffff` .. `#f3efe6`):
  - ✔ USE `#0f172a`, `#18181b`, `#27272a`, or `#7f1d1d` ($|L_c| \ge 82$).
